Company Introduction

One Group. Multiple Industries.
Unlimited Possibilities.

CK Group of Companies is a fast-growing international business group founded by Mr. Chetan Kalirana. The group works across heavy machinery procurement, excavator rental, mining support services, global import-export, China sourcing, medical tourism and technology-based equipment management solutions.

With operations and networks across India, Liberia, Ghana, UAE, China and other global markets, CK Group focuses on practical execution, transparent business processes and long-term partnerships.
